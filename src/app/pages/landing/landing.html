Why OFFVAULT?

Built with privacy as the foundation, not an afterthought.

Fully Offline

Your passwords never leave your device. Everything is stored in your browser's local storage — no servers, no internet required.

Zero Data Collection

No accounts, no analytics, no tracking. OFFVAULT doesn't know you exist, and that's exactly the point.

AES-256-GCM Encrypted

Exports are protected with AES-256-GCM and PBKDF2 key derivation at 100,000 iterations — battle-tested cryptography via the Web Crypto API.

Dual-Key Security

Combine a Secret Key and a Master Password to encrypt your exports. Both are required to unlock the file — two factors, zero compromise.

Secure Import & Export

Back up and sync your vault using encrypted .passlock, .csv, or .xlsx files. Raw (unencrypted) export available too.

Built-In Power Tools

Generate strong passwords instantly, search entries in real-time by key or description, and copy credentials to clipboard with a single click.

How It Works

Three simple steps to secure password management.

1

Open the Vault

Click "Get Started" to access your secure password vault directly in the browser.

2

Add Your Credentials

Enter your key (email, username), password, and an optional description for each entry.

3

Manage Anytime

Edit, delete, or copy passwords with a single click. Everything stays on your device.
